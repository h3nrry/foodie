## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Refined but affordable teppanyaki in the Guanxin business area, listed by iFoodie as a lunch/dinner and date-night pick.

關新商圈的精緻平價鐵板料理，愛食記列為午晚餐及約會推薦。
