## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Long-running local teppanyaki with set meals at accessible prices — over a dozen mains to choose from, and a seafood upgrade for a second main.

在地經營多年的平價鐵板燒，十多種主菜可選，可加價升級第二道海鮮主菜。
