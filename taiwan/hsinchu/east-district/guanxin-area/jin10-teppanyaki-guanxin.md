## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Small, minimalist Japanese-style teppanyaki run by two young chefs; whole-cut meats grilled with light seasoning. Seats fill within minutes of opening, so queue early.

兩位年輕主廚經營的日式文青風鐵板料理，肉品原塊分切、調味清爽少油；開門幾分鐘就客滿，建議提早排隊。
