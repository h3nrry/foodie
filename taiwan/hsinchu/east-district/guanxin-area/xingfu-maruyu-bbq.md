## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Japanese yakiniku bar where staff-grilled or self-grilled à la carte cuts come with salt-and-scallion or tare seasoning; good for late dinners with drinks.

日式燒肉酒場，單點肉品可選鹽蔥或醬燒，適合晚餐小酌聚會。
