## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Italian-leaning chain café serving pasta in white/red/pesto/garlic/spicy sauces, baked rice, thin-crust pizza, and creative small bites — a reliable Western-food pick near Guanxin Park.

義式連鎖餐廳，白醬／紅醬／青醬／蒜香／辣味義大利麵、焗飯與千層披薩，關新公園旁的西式選擇。
