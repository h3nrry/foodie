## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Long-standing Hsinchu restaurant for Peking roast duck "three ways" (一鴨三吃) and handmade Cantonese dim sum; set menus for 2 to 10 people.

新竹老字號，招牌北京烤鴨一鴨三吃與手工港式點心，有 2～10 人套餐。
