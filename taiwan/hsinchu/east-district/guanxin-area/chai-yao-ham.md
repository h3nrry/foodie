## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Butcher-style shop from the 柴窯火腿 ham brand: brick-oven buns, house-cured meats and sauces, best known for thick handmade beef burgers.

柴窯火腿品牌的歐風肉舖，自製窯烤麵包、肉品與醬料，以厚實手工牛肉漢堡聞名。
