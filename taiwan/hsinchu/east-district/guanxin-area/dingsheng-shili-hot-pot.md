## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Two concepts in one building: a buffet-style hot pot on 1F and a Japanese A5 wagyu all-you-can-eat (和牛放題) on 2F, with a vegetable bar, DIY cocktails and Cold Stone ice cream.

同棟兩種餐期：一樓自助式鍋物、二樓日本 A5 和牛吃到飽，附野菜自助吧、DIY 調酒與 Cold Stone 冰淇淋。
