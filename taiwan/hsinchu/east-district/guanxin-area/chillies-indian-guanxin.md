## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Indian restaurant serving North-Indian curries, tandoori grills and fresh naan — popular with the nearby science-park crowd. (The Michelin Guide lists a Taichung sister restaurant, Chilliesine West; the Hsinchu branch itself is not confirmed as a Bib Gourmand.)

道地北印度料理：咖哩、坦都里烤物與現烤烤餅，深受竹科員工喜愛。（米其林指南收錄的是台中姊妹店 Chilliesine West，新竹店本身未確認為必比登。）
