## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Taiwan's best-known budget teppanyaki chain — generous veg, garlic and onion, cooked in front of you; a favourite with visiting tourists.

全台知名平價鐵板燒連鎖，青菜與蒜洋蔥給得大方、現炒現吃，外國遊客也愛。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- Beef / pork / chicken / seafood combos with stir-fried cabbage & bean sprouts 牛、豬、雞、海鮮套餐配炒高麗菜豆芽
- Rice, soup and drinks self-serve at most branches 多數分店白飯湯飲自助
