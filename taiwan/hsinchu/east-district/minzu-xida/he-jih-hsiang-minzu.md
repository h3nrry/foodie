## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

MICHELIN Bib Gourmand. Opened 2015; hand-cut pork braised ~10 hours, famous for the 'sun rice' topped with a runny egg.

米其林必比登。2015 年開業，手切溫體豬慢熬約十小時，招牌太陽飯配半熟蛋。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 太陽飯 'Sun rice' — braised pork rice with soft fried egg
- 自製小魚辣椒菜脯 House anchovy-chili pickled radish (add-on)
