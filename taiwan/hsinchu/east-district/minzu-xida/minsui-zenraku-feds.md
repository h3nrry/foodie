## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Popular no-menu (omakase) teppanyaki chain; ranked #1 in DailyView's high-value omakase teppanyaki list 2023–2026.

人氣無菜單鐵板燒，連續四年（2023–2026）獲網路溫度計高CP值無菜單鐵板燒第一名。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 一龍三吃 Live lobster three ways (seared, lobster-roe omelette, miso soup)
- 日本A4/A5和牛 Japanese A4/A5 wagyu
- 北海道生食級干貝 Hokkaido scallop
- Seasonal fish; favourite ingredients can be requested 3 days ahead
