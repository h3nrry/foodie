## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Part of the long-running Hung Hwa teppanyaki group; two big griddles, fresh ingredients and light, clean seasoning.

紅花鐵板燒集團品牌，兩座大煎台現場料理，食材新鮮、調味清爽。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 美國Choice級無骨牛小排 US Choice boneless short rib (signature)
- 雞腿排 Crispy chicken leg steak
- 白晶蝦 Peeled white shrimp
- 桂圓烏骨雞蛋 Black-chicken egg, fried
