## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

MICHELIN Bib Gourmand. Market stall serving thick rice noodles in a light chicken-bone broth with house-fried shallots.

米其林必比登。市場內攤位，雞骨湯頭清爽、自製油蔥香，粗米粉吸滿湯汁。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 海鮮芋頭米粉湯 Seafood & taro rice-noodle soup (fried sea bass, milkfish balls, shrimp, egg crisps, melt-in-mouth taro)
