## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

All-you-can-eat spicy hot pot with wagyu, seafood pastes and a self-serve bar (Häagen-Dazs included).

麻辣鍋吃到飽，和牛、手打滑類與自助吧（含哈根達斯）。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 特上和牛 Premium wagyu
- 蝦滑 / 花枝滑 Shrimp & squid paste
- 牛筋 Beef tendon
