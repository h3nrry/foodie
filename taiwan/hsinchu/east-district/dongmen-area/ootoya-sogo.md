## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Japanese teishoku (set-meal) chain inside the SOGO/Big City food court, serving grilled fish, tonkatsu, and rice-bowl sets.

SOGO／巨城美食街內的日式定食連鎖店，供應烤魚、豬排與丼飯套餐。
