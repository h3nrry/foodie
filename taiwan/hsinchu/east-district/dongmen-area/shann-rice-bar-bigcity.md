## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Wowprime-group 'new Taiwanese' rice bar with bold, rice-friendly dishes and three bottomless rice choices.

瓦城集團跨菜系新台灣料理，重口味下飯菜、三種米飯吃到飽。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 3.5杯雞 '3.5-cup' chicken with rice blood cake
- 台灣甘蔗蝦 Sugarcane shrimp
- 腐乳空心菜 Water spinach with fermented tofu
