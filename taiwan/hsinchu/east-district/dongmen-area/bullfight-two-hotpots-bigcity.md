## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Budget sukiyaki & hot-pot buffet with a big self-serve bar and soft-serve.

平價壽喜燒吃到飽，自助吧與霜淇淋選擇多。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 壽喜燒鍋 / 蔬果豚骨鍋 Sukiyaki or tonkotsu-veg broth
- 黑毛豬肉 Kurobuta pork (upgrade set)
