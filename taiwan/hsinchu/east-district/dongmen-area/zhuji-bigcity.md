## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Northern-Chinese pies, noodles and fried rice at fair prices — good for families.

北方餡餅、麵食、炒飯，價格實在，適合家庭聚餐。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 牛肉餡餅 Beef pie (must-order)
- 牛肉捲餅 Beef roll
- 蝦仁炒飯 Shrimp fried rice
- 雞盅麵 Chicken soup noodles
