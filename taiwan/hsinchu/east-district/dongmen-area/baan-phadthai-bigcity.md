## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Bold, sour-spicy Thai street food from a brand with repeated Bib Gourmand recognition (per the blogger); good for dates and friends.

酸辣重口味的泰式料理（部落客指品牌多年獲必比登），適合約會或朋友聚餐。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 甘藍手卷 Cabbage wraps
- 炭烤雞肉炒河粉 Char-grilled chicken pad thai
- 碳烤沙嗲雞肉串 Chicken satay
- 椰子冰淇淋 Coconut ice cream
