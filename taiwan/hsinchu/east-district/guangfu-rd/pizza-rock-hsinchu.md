## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

American-Italian fusion pizza chain with a Guangfu Rd location, known for thick-yet-airy crusts in around 25 flavors and cheese-pull paninis.

美式與義式融合的連鎖披薩店，光復路上設有門市，招牌厚實鬆軟餅皮多達25種口味，牽絲帕尼尼也人氣十足。
