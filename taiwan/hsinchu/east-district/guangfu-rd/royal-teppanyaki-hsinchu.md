## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Five-star hotel teppanyaki for celebrations and business dinners; menus change seasonally with premium beef and live seafood.

五星飯店鐵板燒，適合慶生與商務宴請；季節菜單主打頂級牛肉與活海鮮。
