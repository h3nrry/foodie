## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

MICHELIN Bib Gourmand (2025, 2026). 30+ years of smoked-duck dishes; inspectors single out the wok-fried duck rice noodles.

米其林必比登（2025、2026）。在地三十多年的鴨肉料理，評審特別推薦現炒鴨肉米粉。

## Menu highlights / 推薦菜單

- 炒鴨肉米粉 Wok-fried duck rice noodles (Hsinchu thin vermicelli)
- 炒鴨血 Stir-fried duck blood with chives & pickled greens (spice adjustable)
- 鴨肉飯 / 鴨肉炒麵 Duck rice / duck fried noodles
