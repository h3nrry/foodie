## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Long-running Japanese-run ramen shop near Big City: rich tonkotsu broth you can customise (richness, saltiness, noodle firmness) and thick-cut chashu. (Earlier versions of this guide placed it on Jinshan St — that was wrong.)

巨城附近的老牌日式拉麵，濃郁豚骨湯頭可調整濃淡鹹度與麵條軟硬，叉燒厚切。（本指南先前誤列於金山街，已更正。）
