## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Garden-style restaurant on Jinshan St blending Mediterranean and French-Italian influences, popular for its greenery-filled dining space.

金山街花園風餐廳，融合地中海與義法料理風格，綠意盎然的用餐空間深受喜愛。
