## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Small, budget teppanyaki in a Jinshan lane: full sets from NT$180, with free-flow tea and a daily dessert.

金山街巷弄的小型平價鐵板燒，全餐 $180 起，紅茶冬瓜茶無限暢飲並附每日甜點。
