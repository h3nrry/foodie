## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Malaysian (大馬) kitchen known for Hainanese chicken rice, bak kut teh and laksa curry, with kaya butter toast on the side.

馬來西亞料理，招牌海南雞飯、肉骨茶與叻沙咖哩，配咖椰奶油烤吐司。
