## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Hong Kong-style roast-meat (燒臘) canteen: char siu, crispy chicken leg and roast duck over rice, plus fried rice and double-boiled soups. A free daily soup and unlimited sago dessert come with meals.

港式燒臘快餐：蜜汁叉燒、脆皮雞腿、燒鴨飯，另有炒飯與燉湯；用餐附每日例湯與無限西米露。
