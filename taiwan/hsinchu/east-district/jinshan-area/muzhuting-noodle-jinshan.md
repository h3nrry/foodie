## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Hsinchu-born chain of Sichuan Yibin-style 燃麵 ("burning noodles" — dry-tossed with chili oil, peanuts and preserved greens), with wontons in red chili oil; free pickled side dishes and three house chili sauces.

新竹起家的川味燃麵連鎖（宜賓燃麵：紅油、花生、芽菜乾拌），紅油抄手也受歡迎；內用小菜與三種辣醬免費自取。
