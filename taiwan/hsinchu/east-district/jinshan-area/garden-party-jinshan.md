## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

French restaurant tucked in a small Jinshan St alley, serving classic French bistro dishes in an intimate garden-style setting.

藏身金山街巷弄的法式餐廳，供應經典法式小酒館料理，環境如花園般靜謐。
