## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

Noodle shop famous for oversized wontons (each with two shrimp) and spicy 老饕麵; also braised pork rice and frozen wontons to take home.

以每顆包兩隻蝦的大餛飩與香辣老饕麵聞名，另有魯肉飯，可外帶生餛飩。
