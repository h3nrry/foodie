## Why it's recommended / 推薦原因

MICHELIN Bib Gourmand. 20+ year Jiangzhe/Shanghainese restaurant over three floors — good for family and group meals.

米其林必比登。開業二十多年的江浙菜，三層樓空間，適合家庭與公司聚餐。
